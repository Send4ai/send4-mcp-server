# Connect Grok

Back to the [list of assistants](../../README.md#supported-assistants).

## Before you start

AI assistants are on for your workspace unless a Client Admin has switched them off, under
**Settings → Integrations → AI assistants**. See [permissions](../permissions.md).

## Connect

1. Open `grok.com/connectors`, select **New Connector**, then **Custom**.
2. Enter `https://mcp.send4.ai/mcp`.
3. Sign in to Send4 when asked.

Grok's menus change often. If yours look different, see
[xAI's guide to connectors](https://docs.x.ai/grok/connectors).

## Choose what it may do

Send4 shows the account you're signed in to, and only the choices your role and your workspace
allow, already ticked. Untick what Grok shouldn't do, then select **Connect**.

If you leave a box unticked, Grok can't do that. Before it launches a campaign, switches a flow
on or off, or invites someone, Send4 makes it show you a summary and wait for your yes. Whether
Grok asks before other changes depends on Grok's own settings.

## Disconnect

Open **Settings → Integrations → AI assistants** in Send4 and select **Disconnect** beside Grok.
Its access ends immediately.
