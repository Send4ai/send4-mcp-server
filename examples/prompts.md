# Things to ask

Back to the [README](../README.md).

Ask in your own words. You don't need to know a tool's name. Each section below needs the
[permission](../docs/permissions.md) in its heading.

## Read flows, runs, campaigns and usage

- "What does my website form flow do? Answer in five sentences."
- "Which card loses the most people in the quote request flow?"
- "Why did yesterday's conversation from the quote form stop?"
- "How did last week compare with the week before? Give me conversations started and the reply rate."
- "How close are we to our SMS limit this month?"
- "When is my next meeting? Give it in my local time."
- "How is the spring campaign doing?"
- "Are any of my flows failing more often than usual?"
- "Which conversations are waiting for a person to answer?"
- "Find a free 30-minute slot on Thursday afternoon for the customer from this morning's form."
- "Who in the team hasn't accepted their invitation yet?"

## Read conversation messages

- "Read the latest conversation from the website chat and tell me what the customer wanted."
- "Which customers asked about prices this week, and what did the AI answer?"

## Build and change flows

- "Build a draft flow for my contact form: thank the lead by SMS, ask for their address, and book a site visit."
- "Rehearse the new flow as a customer who wants a price before booking."
- "Suggest shorter wording for the first message in my quote flow. Don't change it, I'll accept it myself."
- "Make a copy of the live booking flow with a friendlier opening message, rehearse it, and tell me what changed."

## Create and launch campaigns

- "How many leads tagged *spring-2026* could a campaign reach?"
- "Create a draft campaign for them with the reactivation flow, sending weekdays 9:00–17:00."
- "Check the draft campaign before launch and list anything I should fix."
- "Pause the spring campaign."

## Reply to customers and manage conversations

- "Take over the conversation with the lead from this morning's form, so I can answer myself."
- "Book that customer in for Thursday at 14:00."
- "Hand the conversation back to the AI."

## Invite and manage team members

- "Invite a new agent to the workspace."
- "Resend the invitation to the agent who hasn't accepted yet."
